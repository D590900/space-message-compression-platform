# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Platform engineers, media-infrastructure operators, and organization administrators use the console during focused operational work. They need to understand queue state, compression outcomes, quality evidence, storage artifacts, capsule budgets, and access controls without translating raw API responses by hand.

## Product Purpose

The console is the authenticated operational surface for the Space Message Compression Platform. It turns the API-first system into an inspectable workflow: select an organization and project, submit or follow compression work, compare measured candidates, retrieve verified artifacts, build strict-budget capsules, and manage project policy and credentials according to role. Success means an operator can identify state, risk, and the next valid action quickly while every mutation remains backed by the real control-plane API.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, accountable. The interface should feel mission-critical without becoming theatrical: technically literate, candid about unavailable evidence, and composed under failure or degraded service.

## Anti-references

- Decorative sci-fi control rooms, neon telemetry, star-field backgrounds, and fictional mission language.
- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable rounded cards, oversized vanity metrics, and decorative gradients.
- Terminal cosplay, dense monospace everywhere, or dark mode used merely because the product is technical.
- Claims of quality, readiness, or model capability that are not supported by measured platform evidence.

## Design Principles

- Lead with operational state and the next valid action.
- Make evidence inspectable: provenance, hashes, quality gates, timings, and budget decisions remain available at the point of use.
- Prefer progressive disclosure over visual noise; dense detail is earned by an operator's drill-down.
- Use familiar product affordances and consistent component behavior so the tool disappears into the task.
- Treat unavailable, pending, failed, and partial states as first-class outcomes rather than edge-case copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA across keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, focus, error-identification, target-size, reflow, and reduced-motion requirements. Never encode job or quality state by color alone. Preserve usable information architecture at 200% zoom and from narrow mobile widths through dense desktop workstations.
